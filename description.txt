Terraria Ambience is a mod that adds liveliness to your little Terraria world. You can hear animals, environmental sounds, and more.
This will allow you to relax in your playthroughs more.

What this mod does:

- Reverb in enclosed spaces
- Sound occlusion for blocked sounds
- Audio dampening for underwater audio
- Many footstep sounds for many different material types, with mod support
- Crafting sounds that can be heard by everyone, and everyone can hear
- Sounds for parts of the world that include nature, including the:
	- Forest
	- Desert
	- Corruption/Crimson
	- Underground/Underworld
	- Jungle
	- Ocean
	- Snow/Ice
	- And more!
- Reworks of previously horrendous ambience, such as:
	- Wind
	- Rain
- New audio to make immersion better, such as:
	- Better thunder
	- Better blizzards
	- Campfires

- All aforementioned features work in multiplayer, with InfraSonic support
